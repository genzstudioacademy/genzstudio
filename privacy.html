Privacy Policy

GEN Z STUDIO & ACADEMY respects the privacy of people who contact us through this website.

Information you provide

If you use the enquiry form, the information you enter (such as your name, phone number, selected service and project details) is placed into a WhatsApp message for you to review and send. The website form itself does not submit that information to a website database.

WhatsApp and third-party services

When you choose to open WhatsApp, your interaction is handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy. This website also loads fonts and illustrative images from third-party services when available.

How we use enquiries

Information you choose to send may be used to respond to your enquiry and discuss the requested service. Please do not send passwords, payment card details, identity documents or other sensitive information through the enquiry form.
